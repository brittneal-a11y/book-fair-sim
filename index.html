Book Fair

Walk: tap or click a spot. Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys.

Look: tap an item, or face it and press E / Space.

Stock displays: tap any shelf, table, rack, bin or the checkout, then move items from the box onto it. A ! means it's running low. Tap the door for a new delivery.

Arrange: place, move, rotate and remove furniture. Your room saves automatically.

Helping: a yellow ! means a customer needs help. Tap them to hear what they want, find it on a display or in a box, tap Take to them, then tap them to hand it over.

Fair days: tap Open the fair. Customers browse, then line up at the checkout. Tap the counter to ring them up and count out change. Tap a customer to see what they're looking for.

Arrange shortcuts: R rotate, Delete remove, Esc deselect.
